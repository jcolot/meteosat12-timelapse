24 hours of Earth from Meteosat-12 (MTG-I1), one image every 10 minutes, smoothed with motion-compensated in-between frames. The night side is shown dark with city lights, and the twilight is relit with a physical atmosphere model.

Behind the Earth is the real sky as seen from the satellite: stars at their true positions, the Moon depending on the date, the Milky Way and the zodiacal light. They drift because the satellite turns with the Earth.

Positions are exact. Brightness is enhanced: a camera exposed for the sunlit Earth would show a black sky.
